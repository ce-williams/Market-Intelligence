Near-term read: trend is strong, the next move needs confirmation

U.S. stocks ended a record-setting week close to the S&P 500’s recent high. The advance was uneven: the S&P gained 1.2% on the week while the Russell 2000 fell 0.9%. With the 10-year yield at 5.24% and oil swinging on Iran-related headlines, Wednesday’s inflation print and early earnings guidance are the main tests of whether the rally can broaden.
